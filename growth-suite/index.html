Common Questions

What school leaders ask before getting started.

Can the Growth Suite work with our current systems?

We'll review your existing tools and admissions process during the demo. Integration options depend on the systems involved and the data available.

How does the Growth Suite organize families?

Use tags, custom fields, grade level, inquiry source, engagement, and pipeline stage to build lists and automate relevant follow-up.

Can our team manage email and text in one place?

The platform includes email and SMS workflows, conversation tracking, and team notifications. We'll configure the available channels for your setup.

What does onboarding look like?

We help customize your funnels, forms, pipeline, and automations, then train your team so the system reflects how your school works.

Will we definitely save 15 hours every week?

15+ hours is the time-saving goal the suite is designed around, not a guaranteed outcome for every school. Actual savings depend on your current workflow, adoption, and which tasks you automate.
