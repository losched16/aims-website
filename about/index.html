Where it began

It Started by Listening to Schools.

In 2016, I was working with Independent School Management, helping schools rethink their financial aid policies and put stronger programs in place. My work eventually brought me deeper into marketing and admissions, where I began teaching digital marketing through workshops, live training, and hundreds of webinars.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to coach and mentor thousands of school leaders. And the more time I spent working alongside them, the clearer one thing became: schools didn't lack passion or great programs. They lacked a connected, practical way to reach the right families and guide them through enrollment.

Schools needed more than another disconnected tool or a one-size-fits-all agency playbook.

They needed someone who understood the school first.

Clint presenting at a school leadership event.
The problem we saw

Two Gaps. One Better Way Forward.

AiMS grew out of two recurring frustrations we saw in school marketing and admissions.

THE TECHNOLOGY GAP

Too Many Tools. Not Enough Connection.

Schools were juggling separate platforms for websites, ads, email, forms, and admissions. Their admissions systems often did little to support marketing, while their marketing tools couldn't show which campaigns actually produced enrollments.

THE PARTNERSHIP GAP

Generic Marketing Doesn't Fit a Unique School.

Traditional agencies often arrived with their own playbook. But every school has its own mission, families, programs, culture, and challenges. The best strategy starts with understanding those differences, not forcing a school into somebody else's process.

Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Clint Losch.

For more than 20 years, I've worked in marketing and admissions, including years spent directly with school leadership teams.
