Common Questions

What school leaders usually ask.

Do we need a dedicated marketing person?

No. Done With You works well when a staff member can spend a few hours each week executing with support. Done For You is designed for schools that need AiMS to handle the campaign work.

How is this different from hiring a typical agency?

This is a school-specific system that combines market research, messaging, admissions tools, long-term parent nurturing, training, and implementation. Depending on the engagement, your team can learn to operate it or have ours manage it.

What exactly is in the market study?

A review of competing programs and tuition where verifiable, local demand and demographics, your positioning, potential differentiators, a recommended channel plan, and prioritized actions for the next 30, 60, and 90 days.

What if our school tried marketing software before?

Software is only one component. We pair the Growth Suite with installation, campaigns, follow-up workflows, coaching, and measurement so your team has a clear process for using it.

How soon could we see results?

Timing depends on your market, enrollment season, advertising budget, offer, and response process. We establish a starting baseline and review inquiries, tours, and applications as campaigns run. Past school results are examples, not promises.

How long is the program, and is advertising included?

The core system is designed around a full-year enrollment cycle. The recommended advertising spend is commonly $500–$1,500 a month, paid directly to the ad platform in addition to your AiMS engagement. The exact scope, term, and budget are confirmed before you enroll.

Is this only for Montessori schools?

No. Clint and Tim partnered on the Montessori Foundation version of the program. This AiMS experience is positioned for private, independent, Montessori, and early childhood schools, with strategy and language tailored to each school.
