Common questions

Questions About the Free Audit

Is the marketing audit really free?

Yes. There's no charge to request your free marketing audit. It's designed to give school leaders a helpful starting point.

Do I need to give you access to my accounts?

Not for an initial review of publicly available information. A more detailed assessment of advertising performance or admissions conversions requires data or account access you choose to share.

Will I receive an instant score?

No. Unlike an automated instant grading tool, this is a request for a school-focused review. Our team will follow up about the report.

Is this only for Montessori schools?

No. AiMS supports independent, private, Montessori, faith-based, and specialty schools. The review is tailored to your school and its local market.

Does the report include admissions conversion rates?

The free report focuses on your outward-facing digital marketing. Our Deep Dive Diagnostic can examine inquiry, tour, application, and enrollment conversion rates using your admissions data.
