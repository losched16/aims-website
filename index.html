Why school marketing falls short

Parents Are Still Deciding Long After Your First Follow-Up.

Choosing a school takes time. Families research programs, compare options, read reviews and ask other parents for advice. Niche survey findings indicate that many parents begin researching at least six months ahead and consider several schools.

Yet most enrollment marketing is built around capturing an inquiry and following up a few times. When that communication fades, the family is often still deciding. AiMS helps schools remain helpful and relevant throughout that journey.
