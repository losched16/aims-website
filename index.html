The Challenge

What’s Standing Between Your School and Its Next Enrollment Goal?

Growing enrollment takes more than reaching additional parents. You need to attract families who are the right fit, help them understand what makes your school different, and stay connected throughout their decision. We help schools identify and fix the gaps that get in the way.

  • Attract More Right-Fit Families:Reach parents who value your school's approach, programs, and community instead of paying for inquiries that never become a good fit.
  • Turn More Website Visitors Into Inquiries:Give parents a clear next step with admissions pages built around their questions, from tuition to tours and program-specific information.
  • Get More From Your Advertising Budget:Test the messages and creative parents respond to, then direct spend toward campaigns producing meaningful enrollment opportunities.
  • Build a Year-Long Parent Nurturing System:Keep helping families who are six months or more from deciding, so your school stays top of mind while they compare their options.
  • Improve Tour-to-Application Conversion:Prepare families before visits, answer concerns afterward, and give interested parents a clear path toward applying.
  • Get Found Where Parents Are Searching:Strengthen your school's visibility and story across search, local discovery, and the places families research education options.
  • Respond Faster to New Inquiries:Connect forms, alerts, scheduling, and follow-up so interested parents hear from your school promptly.
  • Make Marketing and Admissions Work Together:See which campaigns generate inquiries, tours, and applications, and give your team a shared view of each family's journey.
  • Give Your Team the Skills and Support to Grow:Combine hands-on implementation with practical marketing and admissions coaching, software, and ongoing professional development.

Who Is
AiMS?

AiMS Growth Partners is a school-focused marketing and admissions growth company based in West Chester, Pennsylvania. We help private, independent, Montessori, and early childhood schools attract and retain their perfect-match families.

Our work combines strategic coaching and training with hands-on implementation. That can include AI-powered advertising, enrollment-focused landing pages, an automated marketing and admissions system, and a full year of helpful parent follow-up. We also bring ongoing professional development through The Circle to support the people responsible for your school's growth.

Whether your school needs help with one urgent challenge or wants a complete enrollment system, we start by understanding your parent journey, identifying what to improve first, and putting the right solutions to work.
