Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know.

Who is The Circle for?

School leaders and administrative staff responsible for enrollment, marketing, admissions, finance, and operations. It is designed for the administrative team, not classroom teacher professional development.

Can my entire school team access The Circle?

Yes. One school membership includes administrative team access, so the school can invite appropriate colleagues without buying individual memberships.

What resources are available?

Playbooks, webinar recordings, courses, worksheets, checklists, frameworks, and growing professional development resources.

What is Circle AI?

A way to discover and explore Circle's educational resources, with references to relevant materials behind its answers.

What can the AI assistants help us with?

First drafts and planning for enrollment, admissions, marketing, family resources, tuition considerations, leadership, and parent communication. Review all outputs before use.

Will there be live workshops and webinars?

The Circle is expanding its live programming with workshops, webinars, and learning opportunities as scheduled.

Can we cancel our membership?

Subscriptions can be managed through the billing portal. Review the applicable billing terms when purchasing.
