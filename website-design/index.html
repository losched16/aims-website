Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know Before Rebuilding.

Can you really launch in three weeks?

Our process is designed for a focused three-week build. We'll confirm a realistic timeline after reviewing the number of pages, content, integrations, and approvals your project requires.

What if we already have a website?

Most schools do. We'll review what should be retained, plan the migration, and keep your existing site available until the replacement is ready to go live whenever the hosting setup allows.

Who writes the copy?

Our team drafts conversion-focused copy based on your school and parent audience. You provide input and approvals rather than starting from a blank document.

What happens after launch?

We can provide ongoing hosting, maintenance, security, content updates, and search-focused improvements based on the support plan you select.

Do we need the Growth Suite?

No. A conversion-focused website can stand on its own. Connecting it to the Growth Suite adds admissions CRM tracking, automated nurturing, and a more complete view of the parent journey.

Will our school own its website?

We'll define asset ownership, platform access, hosting, and handoff terms clearly in your project agreement before work begins.
