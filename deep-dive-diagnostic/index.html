Frequently asked questions

What You May Be Wondering.

Who is this diagnostic for?

Private, independent, Montessori, Christian, classical, boarding, gifted, and other specialty schools that want to understand and improve how they attract and enroll families.

What information do you need from us?

We'll begin with public-facing marketing and website data. To evaluate actual admissions conversion rates, we'll also request aggregate inquiry, tour, application, acceptance, and enrollment information, plus relevant marketing performance data when available. Student-level personal information is not required for this analysis.

What if we don't track every admissions stage?

That's a useful finding in itself. We'll map what is available, identify measurement gaps, and recommend how to track missing stages. We won't invent conversion rates where the data doesn't exist.

Does the diagnostic include implementing the changes?

The $950 engagement covers the assessment and roadmap. Implementation is separate. Your team can use the recommendations independently, or we can discuss help through AiMS services.

How is this different from a free automated marketing audit?

Automated audits report technical scores. Our diagnostic connects marketing, competition, messaging, admissions processes, and conversion data into school-specific decisions and an implementation roadmap.

Will this require us to change our software?

No. We'll review the systems and workflows you already use and recommend improvements based on your goals. Any potential software changes are optional recommendations, not a requirement for the diagnostic.
